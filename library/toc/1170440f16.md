  - CHAPTER 17
  - Yuan Shu despatches seven divisions to attack the east. Cao Cao unites three forces to combat Yuan Shu.
  - CHAPTER 18
  - Jia Xu wins a great victory. Guo Jia gives Cao Cao ten reasons for victory.
  - CHAPTER 19
  - Cao Cao gathers the army at Xiapi. Lü Bu is killed in battle.
  - CHAPTER 20
  - Cao Cao and the emperor go hunting. Dong Cheng is given a secret edict by the emperor
  - CHAPTER 21
  - Cao Cao discusses heroes with Xuande. Yuan Shu dies, and Guan Yu kills Che Zhou to capture Xuzhou.
  - CHAPTER 22
  - Yuan Shao and Cao Cao go to war. Guan Yu and Zhang Fei capture two enemy agents.
  - CHAPTER 23
  - CHAPTER 24
  - Cao Cao murders the emperor’s concubine Lady Dong. Xuande unites with Yuan Shao to attack Cao Cao.
  - CHAPTER 25
  - Guan Yu is captured by Cao Cao’s men but sets three conditions. Cao Cao breaks the siege of Baima.
  - CHAPTER 26
  - CHAPTER 27
  - Guan Yu escorts Xuande’s wives over four hundred miles. He kills six officials en route at five passes.
  - CHAPTER 28
  - Guan Yu kills Cai Yang to show Zhang Fei he is no traitor. The three brothers are united again.
  - CHAPTER 29
  - Sun Ce kills Yu Ji. He dies, and Sun Quan takes over the south – Wu.
  - CHAPTER 30
  - Yuan Shao runs out of food and is defeated. Cao Cao burns down the granary.
  - CHAPTER 31
  - Cao Cao defeats Yuan Shao. Xuande is in despair but finds refuge with Liu Biao.
  - CHAPTER 32
  - Yuan Shao dies, and Yuan Shang struggles to take Jizhou. Cao Cao plans to flood the river and so capture the north.
  - CHAPTER 33
  - Cao Pi seizes his moment to find a wife. Yuan Tan is killed.
  - CHAPTER 34
  - Lady Cai eavesdrops on a secret. Xuande and horse leap a river.
  - CHAPTER 35
  - Xuande encounters a hermit. Xuande starts to search for a master.
  - CHAPTER 36
  - Xuande plots to seize Fancheng. Xu Shu reads a fake letter and leaves, but suggests Kong Ming to Xuande.
  - CHAPTER 37
  - Sima Hui recommends Kong Ming. Xuande tries to see him three times.
  - CHAPTER 38
  - Kong Ming reveals the truth about the three kingdoms. Sun Quan prepares to avenge his father.
  - CHAPTER 39
  - Liu Qi asks three times for help. Kong Ming commands his first battle at Bowang.
  - CHAPTER 40
  - Lady Cai plots to hand over Jingzhou to Cao Cao. Liu Biao dies, and his sons fight, and Kong Ming burns Xinye.
  - CHAPTER 41
  - Xuande leads his people to safety. Zhao Zilong rescues the heir apparent of Xuande.
  - CHAPTER 42
  - Zhang Fei wreaks havoc at the bridge. Xuande retreats in defeat.
  - CHAPTER 43
  - Kong Ming debates with the advisers of Wu. Lu Su goes against the majority opinion.
  - CHAPTER 44
  - Kong Ming pushes Zhou Yu into action. Sun Quan finally decides to attack Cao Cao.
  - CHAPTER 45
  - Cao Cao is defeated at the Three Rivers. Jiang Gan is fooled by a fake letter.
  - CHAPTER 46
  - Kong Ming ‘borrows’ arrows through a clever plot. Huang Gai agrees to be punished to help a secret plan.
  - CHAPTER 47
  - Kan Ze delivers a fake letter. Pang Tong cons Cao Cao into linking his ships.
  - CHAPTER 48
  - Cao Cao holds a banquet on the river and sings a song. The north attacks the south with chained ships.
  - CHAPTER 49
  - CHAPTER 50
  - Kong Ming predicts Cao Cao will try to escape via Huarong. Guan Yu lets Cao Cao escape because of friendship.
  - CHAPTER 51
  - CHAPTER 52
  - Kong Ming justifies capturing three cities. Zhao Zilong uses a clever plot to capture Guiyang.
  - CHAPTER 53
  - Guan Yu frees Huang Zhong in honour of his virtue. Sun Quan fights against Zhang Liao.
  - CHAPTER 54
  - Dowager Empress Wu meets her future son-in-law in a temple. Xuande marries a worthy bride.
  - CHAPTER 55
  - Xuande convinces his wife to flee Wu, and her cunning saves them. Kong Ming drives Zhou Yu to fury for the second time.
  - CHAPTER 56
  - Cao Cao hosts a banquet at the Bronze Bird Tower. Kong Ming drives Zhou Yu to fury a third time.
  - CHAPTER 57
  - Zhou Yu dies, and Kong Ming comes to mourn him. Pang Tong is shunted off to govern at Leiyang.
  - CHAPTER 58
  - Ma Chao raises an army for revenge. Cao Cao not only shaves his beard but sheds his cloak to escape capture.
  - CHAPTER 59
  - Xu Chu fights Ma Chao. Cao Cao sows distrust between Ma Chao and Han Sui.
  - CHAPTER 60
  - Zhang Song tries to convince Cao Cao but is dismissed. Pang Tong convinces Xuande to seize Shu.
  - CHAPTER 61
  - Zhao Zilong rescues Ah Dou from the river. Sun Quan uses a letter to deter Cao Cao.
  - CHAPTER 62
  - Yang and Gao are killed at the fall of the Fu pass. Huang Zhong and Wei Yan compete to capture Luocheng.
  - CHAPTER 63
  - Kong Ming mourns the death of Pang Tong. Zhang Fei frees Yan Yan.
  - CHAPTER 64
  - Kong Ming plots to capture Zhang Ren. Ma Chao’s family are all murdered and Yang Fu sets off to destroy Ma Chao.
  - CHAPTER 65
  - Ma Chao captures Jiameng pass, while Zhang Fei gets annoyed. Xuande takes over Shu.
  - CHAPTER 66
  - CHAPTER 67
  - Cao Cao conquers Hanzhong. Sun Quan escapes.
  - CHAPTER 68
  - Gan Ning attacks Cao Cao’s camp with just a hundred horsemen. Zuo Ci taunts Cao Cao with a flung cup.
  - CHAPTER 69
  - Guan Lu uses the Book of Changes to work out what to do. Five brave men die for their country.
  - CHAPTER 70
  - Zhang Fei captures Wakou Pass by cunning. Old Huang Zhong captures the mountain by stealth.
  - CHAPTER 71
  - Huang Zhong scores another victory. Zhao Zilong defeats a vast army.
  - CHAPTER 72
  - Kong Ming captures Hanzhong by cunning. Cao Cao retreats.
  - CHAPTER 73
  - Xuande becomes King of Hanzhong. Guan Yu captures Xiangyang.
  - CHAPTER 74
  - Pang De takes his coffin on campaign with him. Guan Yu drowns seven enemy forces.
  - CHAPTER 75
  - Hua Tuo operates on Guan Yu’s arm as he plays chess. Lü Meng sails to the capture of Jingzhou.
  - CHAPTER 76
  - Xu Huang fights Guan Yu. Guan Yu retreats.
  - CHAPTER 77
  - CHAPTER 78
  - Hua Tuo treats Cao Cao but is killed. Cao Cao dies.
  - CHAPTER 79
  - Cao Pi, to trick his brother, commands him to create poems. Liu Feng is executed for failing to rescue Guan Yu.
  - CHAPTER 80
  - Emperor Xian is deposed by Cao Pi and he makes himself emperor. Xuande claims he is the only legitimate Han emperor.
  - CHAPTER 81
  - Zhang Fei is murdered. To avenge his two brothers Xuande goes to war.
  - CHAPTER 82
  - Sun Quan surrenders to Wei. Xuande rewards his men for the war against Wu but upsets Huang Zhong.
  - CHAPTER 83
  - Xuande seizes his personal enemies at Xiaoting. Death of Gan Ning.
  - CHAPTER 84
  - Lu Xun burns down all his enemy’s camps. Kong Ming uses the Eight Maze Puzzle but is defeated.
  - CHAPTER 85
  - Xuande dies, leaving his son in the care of Kong Ming. Kong Ming disarms five armies.
  - CHAPTER 86
  - Deng Zhi shows courage and convinces Sun Quan. Xu Sheng destroys Cao Pi’s army.
  - CHAPTER 87
  - Kong Ming goes to war against the Man people. The king of the Man suffers his first defeat.
  - CHAPTER 88
  - Crossing the Lu River, Meng Huo, the king of the Man, is captured for the second time. Then for the third time.
  - CHAPTER 89
  - Kong Ming tricks and captures Meng Huo a fourth time. Then a fifth time.
  - CHAPTER 90
  - CHAPTER 91
  - Kong Ming offers sacrifices at the Lu River and takes his army home. Cao Pi dies.
  - CHAPTER 92
  - Zhao Zilong kills five Wei officials. Kong Ming takes three cities by cunning.
  - CHAPTER 93
  - Jiang Wei surrenders to Kong Ming. By words alone Kong Ming causes Wang Lang to die.
  - CHAPTER 94
  - Kong Ming defeats the Qiang tribes in a snowstorm. Sima Yi swiftly defeats Meng Da.
  - CHAPTER 95
  - By ignoring Wang Ping’s advice, Ma Su loses Jieting. Just playing a lute, Kong Ming defeats Sima Yi.
  - CHAPTER 96
  - Torn by grief, Kong Ming has to execute Ma Su. Cutting his hair, Zhou Fang fools Cao Xiu.
  - CHAPTER 97
  - Kong Ming urges an attack on Wei. Zhao Zilong dies, and Jiang Wei defeats Wei by the use of a letter.
  - CHAPTER 98
  - CHAPTER 99
  - Kong Ming wins a major battle. Sima Yi invades Shu.
  - CHAPTER 100
  - Cao Zhen is overwhelmed by a Shu attack on his camp. Kong Ming shames Sima Yi but is recalled and humbled.
  - CHAPTER 101
  - Kong Ming pretends to be a god. Zhang He falls into a trap.
  - CHAPTER 102
  - Sima Yi captures the bridge over Beiyuan. Guan Xing dies and Kong Ming tricks Sima Yi again.
  - CHAPTER 103
  - CHAPTER 104
  - Kong Ming dies – a great star falls. A wooden statue frightens Sima Yi.
  - CHAPTER 105
  - Kong Ming leaves a plan in a silk bag. Cao Rui ruthlessly seizes the Dew Collection Bronze Bowl.
  - CHAPTER 106
  - Cao Rui dies, and Cao Shuang usurps power for ten years. Faking ill health, Sima Yi sets a trap for Cao Shuang.
  - CHAPTER 107
  - Sima Yi takes over. Jiang Wei loses a battle.
  - CHAPTER 108
  - Sima Yi dies and Sun Quan dies. Sun Jun executes a treacherous plot at a banquet.
  - CHAPTER 109
  - The emperor of Wei is deposed. Cao Mao becomes emperor.
  - CHAPTER 110
  - Wen Yang defeats an army on his own. Sima Shi dies, and while Jiang Wei wins a battle he is then defeated.
  - CHAPTER 111
  - Zhuge Dan raises an army. He battles Sima Zhao.
  - CHAPTER 112
  - Sima Zhao tricks Zhuge Dan. Jiang Wei fights to seize Changcheng.
  - CHAPTER 113
  - Ding Feng plots to kill Sun Chen, while Sun Liang is deposed. Jiang Wei defeats Deng Ai but is betrayed at home.
  - CHAPTER 114
  - Emperor Cao Mao is murdered in his carriage. Jiang Wei outwits Deng Ai.
  - CHAPTER 115
  - The second ruler listens to rumours and recalls the army. Jiang Wei goes to take charge of the army colonies.
  - CHAPTER 116
  - A shaman fools the second ruler. Kong Ming’s spirit appears to Zhong Hui.
  - CHAPTER 117
  - Deng Ai scales the mountains to bypass the Shu army. Zhuge Zhan dies fighting.
  - CHAPTER 118
  - Crying at the ancestral shrine, a loyal prince dies. Moving into Shu, two generals compete.
  - CHAPTER 119
  - Jiang Wei plots revenge. A second abdication imitates the first, and Sima Yan becomes emperor.
  - CHAPTER 120
  - Recommending Du Yu, Yang Hu dies. Sun Hao surrenders to Wei, and the Three Kingdoms are united into One.
