# Misconceptions: p-values, confidence intervals and power

On the pretest the learner chose a wrong reading of a p-value, a wrong reading of a 95% confidence interval, and a wrong definition
of power, and skipped the multiple-testing question. This is the core of frequentist inference and the center of the mission
(A/B tests, model evaluation), so it's the first teaching priority. Expect the classic confusions: P(data | H0) vs P(H0 | data),
and "95% probability the parameter is in this interval".

**Evidence:** pretest 2026-10-05: missed ht-pvalue, est-ci, ht-power; skipped ht-multiple.
**Implications:** teach p-value → CI → power → multiple testing, in that order; bridge from the base-rate problem they got right.
