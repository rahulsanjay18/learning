## Course description
An optional conversation at the end of Level II, like the Level I conversation but over graduate reading. You write a list of
works with a short rationale for how they fit together, and we talk for an hour.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Explain how the graduate works you read connect, across theory, methods and applications.
2. Defend a view on an open question in statistics or ML theory.
3. Describe how your understanding changed from Level I to Level II.

## Texts
- **Required:** your own list of works.
