## Course description
An optional capstone: a report of about 20 pages on a question you choose, analysed with the methods from Level I. I advise you
the way a thesis advisor would. We agree on the question and the data first, then outline the report, read drafts, and finish
with a final version you could show an employer or post publicly.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Frame a question that matters to you and that available data can answer.
2. Carry out an analysis with appropriate methods, checks and sensitivity analyses.
3. Write a report that someone outside statistics can follow and someone inside it can check.
