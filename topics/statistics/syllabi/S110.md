## Course description
A review of first-course probability: counting, conditional probability, Bayes' rule, the standard distributions, expectation,
and the limit theorems. On October 6, 2026 it was folded into the Chapter 1 pretest of S201, and you passed it there. It stays on
the course list because S201 lists it as a prerequisite.

## Learning objectives
1. Count outcomes with and without order and replacement.
2. Compute conditional probabilities and use Bayes' rule.
3. Recall the formula, mean and variance of the common distributions.
4. Use linearity of expectation and indicator variables.

## Texts
- **Recommended:** Blitzstein and Hwang, *Introduction to Probability*, 2nd ed. (free from the authors), Chapters 1–4;
  Bertsekas and Tsitsiklis, *Introduction to Probability* (in your library).
