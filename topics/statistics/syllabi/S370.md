## Course description
An optional course on survey sampling: how to learn about a population from a sample you design yourself, and how to correct for
what goes wrong (unequal probabilities, clusters, people who don't respond). It matters any time your data isn't a simple random
sample, which is almost always.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Compute estimates and standard errors under simple random, stratified and cluster sampling.
2. Use ratio and regression estimation to improve precision with auxiliary data.
3. Weight a sample with unequal selection probabilities (Horvitz–Thompson).
4. Explain nonresponse bias and adjust for it with weighting classes or raking.
5. Say what a design effect is and estimate one.

## Texts
- **Required:** Lohr, *Sampling: Design and Analysis* (CRC; 3rd ed., 2021; to buy): the chapters on simple probability samples, stratified sampling, ratio and regression estimation, cluster sampling, unequal-probability sampling, and nonresponse.
