## Course description
An optional self-check modeled on the first-year qualifying exams in statistics PhD programs. There are two timed, closed-book
papers, one on probability and one on theoretical statistics, each followed by a review. It tells you whether Level II stuck.
Nothing depends on the result.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Solve qualifying-exam probability problems (measure theory, limit theorems, martingales) under time pressure.
2. Solve qualifying-exam theory problems (estimation, testing, asymptotics) under time pressure.
3. Identify your weakest topics from the results and plan a review.

## Texts
- **Required:** none new. Practice problems are drawn from the S501–S513 textbooks and from past qualifying exams that universities publish.
