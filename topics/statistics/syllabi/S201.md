## Course description
Chapter 1 of Casella and Berger: probability from first principles. We cover sets and sigma-algebras, Kolmogorov's axioms and what
follows from them, counting, conditional probability and independence, random variables, distribution functions, and density and
mass functions. Your pretest showed the calculations are solid, so this course spends its time on definitions and proofs. Every
later chapter uses them.

You took the Chapter 1 pretest on October 6. Section 1.1 is skipped. The other sections teach only what the pretest showed was
missing. The course ends with a one-page summary of the chapter and a chapter check.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. State Kolmogorov's axioms and prove basic consequences from them alone, such as P(Aᶜ) = 1 − P(A), monotonicity, and Bonferroni's inequality.
2. Count outcomes in the four sampling schemes (ordered or not, with or without replacement) and explain each formula.
3. Tell pairwise from mutual independence, with a counterexample, and derive Bayes' rule, stating what it assumes.
4. Explain what a random variable is (a function on the sample space) and what distribution it induces.
5. State the three properties of a distribution function and prove that it is nondecreasing.
6. Tell a mass function from a density, find a normalizing constant, and go between a density and its distribution function.
