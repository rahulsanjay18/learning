Course description

An optional research practicum. You pick a published statistics or ML-theory paper, reproduce its main result (a simulation, a proof or an analysis), and then try one extension of your own. It's the step from reading research to doing it.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Read a research paper closely enough to reproduce its main result.
  2. Write reproducible code, or a complete proof, for that result.
  3. Propose and test one extension, and report what happened, including a negative result.

Texts

  • Required: the paper you choose.
