Course description

An optional Level II capstone: a paper of about 40 pages, along the lines of a master's thesis. It can be methodological (a new method or a new analysis of an existing one), applied (a substantial analysis with real stakes), or theoretical. I advise it the way a thesis advisor would, with a proposal, regular drafts, and a final version you could submit to a workshop or post as a preprint.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Frame an open question and place it in the existing literature.
  2. Carry out original work with rigor appropriate to the question.
  3. Write it up to the standard of a workshop paper.
