Course description

An optional graduate elective on nonparametric statistics: estimating curves and distributions without assuming a parametric form. It covers the empirical distribution function, the bootstrap and jackknife, smoothing and the bias–variance tradeoff, nonparametric regression and density estimation, and minimax rates.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Use the empirical distribution function and the DKW inequality to build confidence bands.
  2. Choose a bandwidth for kernel smoothing by cross-validation, and derive the bias and variance of a kernel estimator.
  3. Fit local polynomial and spline regressions and compare them.
  4. Estimate a density with kernels and histograms and give the optimal convergence rate.
  5. Explain minimax rates for smooth function classes.

Texts

  • Required: Wasserman, All of Nonparametric Statistics (Springer, 2006; to buy): Chapter 2 "Estimating the CDF and Statistical Functionals", Chapter 3 "The Bootstrap and the Jackknife", Chapter 4 "Smoothing: General Concepts", Chapter 5 "Nonparametric Regression", Chapter 6 "Density Estimation", Chapter 7 "Normal Means and Minimax Theory".
  • Optional: Tsybakov, Introduction to Nonparametric Estimation (Springer, 2009; to buy), for rigorous rates.
