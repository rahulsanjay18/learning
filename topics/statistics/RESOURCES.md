# Statistics Resources

## Knowledge

**Primary source for the course (learner's choice, 2026-10-05): Casella & Berger** (grade A). _All of Statistics_ is the
companion read (grade B). Point every lesson at a Casella & Berger section, plus the matching AoS section when there is one.

- Book: _All of Statistics: A Concise Course in Statistical Inference_ by Larry Wasserman (Springer, 2004) (in collection, grade B, id `051a7877e5`)
  Fast, modern, ML-friendly tour: probability (ch. 1–5), inference, bootstrap, MLE, testing (ch. 6–10), Bayesian (11),
  decision theory (12), regression (13), multivariate (14). Grade B: teach from prose and point to sections; **never copy its
  equations** from the conversion. /toc is empty; the book's contents page is near line 380–700, find sections with /search.
  Use for: the shorter companion pointer in every lesson.
- Book: _Statistical Inference_, 2nd ed., by George Casella & Roger L. Berger (Duxbury, 2002; ISBN 0-534-24312-6) (in collection, grade A, id `f9dc4d1d4c`)
  The standard graduate-level mathematical statistics text: probability, distributions, estimation, testing, intervals.
  Use for: the rigorous version of each lesson; equations may be taken from it (grade A, re-check). Its /toc is broken, so find sections with `/search` (e.g., §8.3.4 p-Values is near line 22000).
- Book: _Practical Statistics for Data Scientists_ by Peter Bruce, Andrew Bruce & Peter Gedeck (in collection, grade A, id `a5650effae`)
  Applied statistics for data work, with R and Python: sampling, testing, regression, classification. Use for: the career angle, A/B tests.
- [Book: _Introductory Statistics 2e_ (OpenStax, free)](https://openstax.org/details/books/introductory-statistics-2e) (in collection, grade B, id `1d472f742f`; the web version has clean math)
  Gentle intro-course coverage. Use for: quick refreshers on anything the pretest shows is rusty.
- [Book: _An Introduction to Statistical Learning_ (free PDF from the authors)](https://www.statlearning.com/) (in collection as grade C; use the authors' site)
  Bridge from statistics to ML: regression, classification, resampling, regularization. Use for: the ML-facing half of the mission.
- [Interactive: _Seeing Theory_ (Brown University)](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/)
  Visual, interactive probability and statistics. Use for: intuition on distributions, CLT, Bayesian updating.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Cross Validated (stats.stackexchange.com)](https://stats.stackexchange.com/)
  Heavily moderated Q&A run by statisticians. Use for: "is this analysis right?" questions; read top answers on p-values and CIs.
- [r/statistics](https://www.reddit.com/r/statistics/)
  Discussion among practitioners and students. Use for: career and methods discussion; lower signal than Cross Validated.
