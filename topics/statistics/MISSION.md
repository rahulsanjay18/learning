# Mission: Statistics

> Updated 2026-10-05 (learner's decision): cover all of Casella & Berger, doing proofs yourself. Plan: `PROGRAM.md`.

## Why
Statistics you can *use* in your AI/ML work: reason correctly about experiments, model evaluation and uncertainty instead of
pattern-matching formulas. It's also the foundation for the ML/AI topics and the AWS ML-track certification you want.

## Success looks like
- Design and analyze an A/B test end to end (sample size and power, the test, multiple comparisons) without looking anything up.
- Read a p-value or confidence interval correctly and spot the common misreadings in other people's work.
- Fit, interpret and critique a regression; tell correlation from causation and name the likely confounders.
- Do simple Bayesian updating (conjugate priors) and say when it beats the frequentist answer.
- Work through all of Casella & Berger, *Statistical Inference*, and write its key proofs yourself.

## Constraints
- At least 1 hour a day across *all* topics; statistics gets part of it, so lessons stay short.
- Math degree that included some statistics: start from a placement pretest, skip what's already solid.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Measure-theoretic probability (Casella & Berger don't need it). Proofs are now **in** scope (changed 2026-10-05, learning record 0006).
