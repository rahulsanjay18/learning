# Military Strategy Resources

Grades refer to `library/MANIFEST.csv` (A = teach and quote; B = prose only, no equations, figures or tables).

## Knowledge

### Core (in collection)
- _Military Strategy: A Very Short Introduction_, Antulio J. Echevarria II (OUP 2017). (in collection, grade A, id 940de01b70)
  The course backbone. Short, current, organized by strategy type (annihilation → cyber), with campaign examples. The author edits the
  US Army War College's _Parameters_ ([SSI profile](https://ssi.armywarcollege.edu/Who-We-Are/Faculty-Staff/Article/3356184)).
  Use for: definitions, the taxonomy, worked historical cases.
- _On War_, Carl von Clausewitz. (in collection, grade A, id 8cb37bf97d; headings are weak, so grep for it.) Free Graham translation:
  [ClausewitzStudies.org Bk1 Ch1](https://clausewitzstudies.org/readings/OnWar1873/BK1ch01.html) · [Project Gutenberg](https://gutenberg.org/files/1946/1946-h/1946-h.htm).
  Use for: war and policy, friction, the trinity, attack vs. defense.
- _Clausewitz: A Very Short Introduction_, Michael Howard. (in collection, grade A, id c589beef4b)
  A short guide by one of On War's standard translators. Use for: reading alongside On War.
- _Strategy: A History_, Lawrence Freedman. (in collection, grade A, id e47c5dab8b)
  Strategy from antiquity through business and revolution. Use for: comparing theorists and their contexts; the transfer-to-other-fields angle.
- _Modern Strategy_, Colin S. Gray. (in collection, grade A, id 5727374d00)
  Gray's general theory: strategy as the "bridge" between policy and military means. Use for: theory lessons and the dimensions of strategy.
- _The Direction of War_, Hew Strachan. (in collection, grade A, id 47014c7c60)
  Contemporary strategy in historical perspective; civil-military "strategic dialogue". Use for: modern wars, Iraq and Afghanistan era.
- _The Art of War_, Sun Tzu, trans. Lionel Giles (1910). (in collection, grade A, id 3329556537)
  Classic translation (public domain). Use for: Sun Tzu's own words; compare with how Liddell Hart borrowed him.
- _The Peloponnesian War_, Thucydides, trans. Hammond (Oxford World's Classics). (in collection, grade A, id 6ff2f46904)
  Use for: the oldest full case study; sea power vs. land power, alliance politics, Athenian strategy.

- _The Rise of the Roman Empire_, Polybius, trans. Ian Scott-Kilvert (Penguin Classics). (in collection, grade A, id a1de43a246)
  The main ancient source for the First Punic War (Book I) and the Hannibalic war (Book III); Book VI compares the Roman and Carthaginian militaries.
  Use for: Punic Wars case studies, primary-source practice.

### Supporting (in collection)
- _Ancient Warfare_, _The Napoleonic Wars_, _The First World War_ (Howard), _World War II_ (Weinberg), _The Cold War_, _Nuclear Weapons_,
  _Aerial Warfare_, _War and Technology_: Oxford VSIs. (in collection, grade A) Use for: compact history background before analyzing a campaign.
- _Game Theory: A Very Short Introduction_, Ken Binmore. (in collection, grade A, id e0ec5b5fef) Use for: the AI/decision-theory bridge.
- _Game Theory 101: The Rationality of War_, William Spaniel. (in collection, **grade B**: prose only, never take equations from it; id 3a2eda2808)
  Use for: bargaining models of war, as a pointer to sections to read in the original.

### Free online
- [_A Discourse on Winning and Losing_, John R. Boyd, ed. Grant Hammond (Air University Press, 2018)](https://www.airuniversity.af.edu/Portals/10/AUPress/Books/B_0151_Boyd_Discourse_Winning_Losing.pdf)
  Boyd's own briefings, OODA loop included. Free PDF from the publisher. Use for: dislocation, tempo, maneuver warfare.
- [War Room (US Army War College): "Lykke's Little Three-Legged Stool"](https://warroom.armywarcollege.edu/podcasts/lykke-stool-pt-2/)
  On Lykke's 1989 ends-ways-means model and its critics. Use for: the analysis framework.

## Wisdom (Communities)
- [r/WarCollege](https://www.reddit.com/r/WarCollege/)
  About 100k members; credible military history and science, and expects sources. Has a reading list. Its rule 1 bans topics from the last
  year, so post historical analyses, not current events. Use for: getting your campaign analyses critiqued.
- [PAXsims](https://paxsims.wordpress.com/) and the Connections conferences ([Connections UK](https://professionalwargaming.co.uk/))
  Hub for professional wargaming and serious games. Use for: the wargaming side of the mission.
