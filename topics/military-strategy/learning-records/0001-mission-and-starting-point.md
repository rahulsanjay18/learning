# Mission set; starting with the capacity-vs-will taxonomy

The learner wants to analyze campaigns (history and current conflicts), use strategy in games and wargames, and link it to
game theory and AI. Theory first, but tactics, weapons, nuclear and modern conflicts are all in scope. They asked for
reading-heavy sessions. They have no stated prior study of military strategy, so we start from Echevarria's definition
and the four basic types. See [[MISSION.md]].

**Implications**: lesson 1 teaches classification (annihilation/dislocation/attrition/exhaustion). The learner's own
"take it outside" campaign analysis is the diagnostic for choosing lesson 2.
