# Mission: English (draft, 2026-10-08; correct me)

## Why
Read the major works of literature in English, with the tools to read them well, and write better along the way. A "good for the soul" major (my guess). Designed, not started.

## Success looks like (end of Level I)
- Explain what a work is doing and how, in your own words, with evidence from it.
- Pick up an unfamiliar piece in the field and know where it sits and how to approach it.

## Constraints
- Parked until a slot opens (one in, one out). No pretests: credit comes from what you've taken; ask or look up when a gap shows.

## Open questions for you
1. Is the "why" right?
2. What have you already done in this subject (courses, instruments, reading)? It moves the entry point.
3. When it starts: half subject (1 lesson a week) or full (2)?
