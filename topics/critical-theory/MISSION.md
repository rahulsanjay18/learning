# Mission: Critical Theory (draft, 2026-10-08; correct me)

## Why
From the request (2026-10-08): learn the lenses by which a text can be analyzed, the factual and theoretical background behind
each one, and read books to apply them to, with the Frankfurt School as part of the background. A "good for the soul" major
with no deadline, explicitly **not for now**.

## Success looks like (end of Level I)
- Pick up any novel, film or article and read it through three or four lenses on purpose, saying what each one shows and misses.
- Read a piece of published criticism (or a culture-war argument about "critical theory") and name the lens, test its evidence,
  and say whether it's being used well.
- Know where each lens comes from: the philosophy, sociology, psychology and history it rests on.

## Constraints
- Parked until a slot opens (one in, one out; `programs.json`). When it starts, it's probably a half subject, so expect a long
  program (see PROGRAM.md TL;DR) unless you give it daily time.
- Reading-heavy by design. Readings are cut into ~25-minute slices.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Original languages (German, French): translations only.
- Writing for publication; the lens essays are for learning.
