Is this piece safe? The two-question shortcut

  1. Is it attacked by an enemy piece worth less? If so, it is not safe.
  2. Does it have at least as many defenders as attackers? If not, it is not safe.

If either check fails, or you're unsure, run the tally.

The tally (the real answer)

  • Each side recaptures with its cheapest piece that can reach the square.
  • Write the running score after every capture.
  • Either side may stop whenever continuing would cost it more.
  • Equal counts can still lose material: Nxe5 dxe5 is −2.
  • A defender too valuable to recapture (often the queen) doesn't really count.

Hope Chess vs. Real Chess (Heisman)

Hope Chess: moving without looking at the opponent's forcing replies (checks, captures, threats) and hoping you'll have an answer.

Real Chess: before each move, checking that the opponent has no forcing reply you can't meet.

Heisman, “Passive vs. Basic Hope Chess”, Chess.com, 2018.

Before every move

“After this move, can my opponent capture anything of mine and come out ahead?”
