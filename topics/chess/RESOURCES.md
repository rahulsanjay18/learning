# Chess Resources

## Knowledge

### Tactics and safety (current focus)
- Book: _Winning Chess Tactics_ by Yasser Seirawan & Jeremy Silman (in collection, grade A, id `50634c974d`)
  Clear, level-appropriate tactics primer: definitions, double attack, pin, skewer, deflection, batteries. Use for: every
  tactics lesson; the point-count glossary; the "counting vs. looking" warning (Ch. 2, p. 12).
- [Article: "Passive vs. Basic Hope Chess" by CM Dan Heisman (Chess.com, 2018)](https://www.chess.com/article/view/passive-vs-basic-hope-chess)
  Defines Hope Chess vs. Real Chess, and why players rated 1200–1700 still play "passive" Hope Chess. Use for: the
  thinking-process habit (checks, captures, threats).
- [Index: Dan Heisman's articles by subject](https://www.danheisman.com/articles-by-subject.html)
  Heisman's public Chess.com columns, sorted by topic. Use for: thought process, time use, safety, how adults improve.
- Book: _Is Your Move Safe?_ by Dan Heisman (Mongoose Press, 2016; CJA Best Instruction book 2016) (check: [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20250577W))
  150+ positions on whether a move is safe; counting attackers and defenders, and when counting doesn't apply. Use for: the safety-check skill.
- Book: _A Guide to Chess Improvement: The Best of Novice Nook_ by Dan Heisman (check: [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL16962920W))
  The best of his award-winning column for improving adults. Use for: study plans and thinking process.
- Book: _Winning Chess Combinations_ by Seirawan (in collection, grade A, id `9d40d07e91`). Use for: after basic tactics.
- Book: _1000 Checkmate Combinations_ by Victor Henkin (in collection, grade B: figures unreliable)
  and _Chess: 5334 Problems, Combinations and Games_ by László Polgár (in collection, grade B: figures unreliable).
  Use for: puzzle drill from the learner's own copy only. Never take diagrams from the conversions.
- [Video: "Counting Material" by IM Alex Astaneh (Lichess video library)](https://lichess.org/mg/video/foX9cljqIv8)
  Short video on counting an exchange on one square. Use for: an optional re-explanation of lesson 1.
- [Lichess Learn and Practice](https://lichess.org/learn), free. Use for: interactive drills (piece value, basic checkmates).

### Strategy and understanding (mission priority #1, after safety)
- Book: _Winning Chess Strategies_ by Seirawan & Silman (in collection, grade A, id `06226b8066`)
  Entry-level strategy: space, pawn structure, good/bad pieces. Use for: the first strategy lessons.
- Book: _How to Reassess Your Chess_, 4th ed., by Jeremy Silman (in collection, grade A, id `9769daad24`)
  The imbalances method: thinking about a position through its differences. Use for: "why is this move good?" Around 1300+.
- Book: _Chess Fundamentals_ by J. R. Capablanca (in collection, grade A, id `d2e32f3e7e`)
  Classic first principles from a world champion: endings first, then middlegame and openings. Use for: endgame basics, piece value nuance.
- Book: _Winning Chess Endings_ by Seirawan (in collection, grade A, id `b7c9d62e2b`). Use for: basic endgames.
- Book: _My System_ by Aron Nimzowitsch (in collection, grade A, id `725d5b0db7`)
  Foundational positional theory (open files, outposts, prophylaxis). Use for: later, once Silman is comfortable.
- Book: _Play Winning Chess_ by Seirawan (in collection, grade B). Use for: prose overview of the elements (force, time, space, pawns).

## Wisdom (Communities)
- [r/chessbeginners](https://www.reddit.com/r/chessbeginners/)
  Large, beginner-friendly subreddit. Use for: posting a game for review, "why was this a blunder?"
- [Lichess forum: General Chess Discussion](https://lichess.org/forum/general-chess-discussion)
  Active and free. Use for: improvement-plan questions.
- [Chess.com "For Beginners" forum](https://www.chess.com/forum/category/for-beginners)
  Use for: questions while playing on Chess.com.
- Local: [US Chess club search](https://new.uschess.org/club-search-and-affiliate-directory)
  Over-the-board clubs; also check Meetup. Use for: casual and rated games against people (mission #3), slow games with post-mortems.
