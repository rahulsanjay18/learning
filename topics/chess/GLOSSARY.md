# Chess Glossary

The canonical words for this workspace. Lessons, reviews and learning records use these terms.

## Material

**Point count**:
The rough value of each piece in pawns: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9; the king is never traded.
Rules of thumb, not laws: the position can change what a piece is really worth.
_Avoid_: piece score, material score

**Exchange (of material)**:
A sequence of captures on one square, in which each side recaptures with its cheapest piece and either side may stop.
_Avoid_: trade-off, swap-off. (Not the same as "the Exchange", capitalised: a rook for a minor piece.)

**Tally**:
The running material score written after each capture of an exchange; the real answer to "who comes out ahead?"
_Avoid_: counting heads (that's the two-question shortcut, not the tally)

**Hanging**:
A piece that can be captured with a net loss of material for its owner, either because it's attacked by something cheaper or because it has more attackers than defenders.
_Avoid_: en prise, loose (loose = undefended, which isn't always hanging)

## Tactics

**Discovered attack**:
An attack by a piece that didn't move: the moved piece got out of its line. Check for one with step 2 of "what did their move do?" (lesson 3).
_Avoid_: hidden attack

**Pin**:
A piece that can't move (or shouldn't) because a more valuable piece stands behind it on the same line. Against the king the pin is
absolute: moving is illegal (41. Rd2 in the 6 October game). Full lesson: G101 0006.
_Avoid_: stuck piece
