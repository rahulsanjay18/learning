# Mission: Chess

## Why
Understand chess deeply, so I know *why* a move is good rather than memorizing which move. Turn that
understanding into better results in online games (Chess.com). Secondary: enjoy casual games with people, and
connect the game to my AI work (engines, search, evaluation).

## Success looks like
- I can explain the reasoning behind a move in my own games (material, safety, plans), not just name the move.
- I stop losing games to pieces left hanging: a safety check before every move becomes a habit.
- My online rating climbs steadily from under 1200.
- I can read a strategy book (Silman, Nimzowitsch) and connect its ideas to positions in my own games.
- Later: I can explain how an engine's search and evaluation relate to the way a human thinks about a position.

## Constraints
- 20–30 minutes per session.
- Inattentive ADHD: short lessons, one idea at a time, immediate feedback.
- Currently rated under ~1200 online.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Opening theory beyond basic principles.
- Engine programming / ML for chess. Revisit once the fundamentals are solid.
