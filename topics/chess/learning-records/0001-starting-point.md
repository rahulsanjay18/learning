# Starting point: online player under 1200, wants understanding first

The learner says they are rated under ~1200 online (Chess.com). Their priorities are deep understanding first, then
winning online games; casual play and the AI/engine angle also matter. Sessions run 20–30 minutes.

**Evidence**: self-reported in the opening interview (2026-10-02). Not yet confirmed by a game.

**Implications**: start with material safety (counting one square, Hope Chess vs. Real Chess). At this level most
games are decided by hanging material, and strategy can't be judged until pieces stop hanging. Frame each tactic as
the reason behind a move, not a trick. Use the learner's first untimed bot game (PGN) to confirm or correct this
starting point before lesson 2.
