# NASM-CPT Resources

## Knowledge

### Official (highest trust: the exam is written from these)
- [Doc: NASM CPT Exam Blueprint (PDF, 2019 job analysis, updated 2.10.2021)](https://www.nasm.org/content/dam/nasm/docs/nasmlibraries/pdf/cpt7-nasm-exam-blueprint.pdf)
  The six domains, their weights, and every knowledge item (K1–K83) the exam can test. Use for: deciding what to study
  and in what proportion. Summarized in `reference/exam-blueprint.html`.
- [Page: NASM CPT Exam Details](https://www.nasm.org/certified-personal-trainer-exam-info)
  Format (120 questions, 2 hours, scaled score 70+), links to the Candidate Handbook. Use for: logistics.
- [Page: The NASM OPT Model](https://www.nasm.org/certified-personal-trainer/the-opt-model)
  NASM's own summary of the 5 phases with rep ranges, sets and %1RM. Use for: OPT lessons. Current (7th-ed.) naming.
- [Blog: The NASM OPT Model: A Trainer's Guide to Programming Clients](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/training/nasm-optimum-performance-training)
  Levels → phases mapping and why Phase 1 comes first. Use for: OPT lessons.
- [Podcast: NASM-CPT 7 Study Guide series (NASM blog)](https://blog.nasm.org/nasm-cpt-7-study-guide-part-i)
  NASM's free audio walk-through of the 7th-edition content, domain by domain. Use for: commute review; good for ADHD-friendly passive pass before a lesson.
- Book: _NASM Essentials of Personal Fitness Training_, **7th ed.** (the exam's textbook) (check: [NASM product page](https://nasm.org/products/cpt-textbook)) — not in collection; on library/WANTED.md.
- Book: _NASM Essentials of Personal Fitness Training_, 4th ed. (2012), Clark, Lucett & Sutton (in collection, grade B, id `9166ed3226`)
  Same core model (OPT, assessments, kinetic chain), older edition. Use for: prose explanations only; check every claim against
  nasm.org for 7th-edition changes. Grade B: never take tables/figures from the conversion.
- Book: _Study Guide to Accompany NASM Essentials of Corrective Exercise Training_ (2010) (in collection, grade A, id `e70ad7cf0e`)
  Use for: overhead squat assessment and over/underactive muscle drills (Domain 3).

### Exam practice
- NASM course practice exam / EDGE app "CPT Exam Prep" (600+ questions) — paid, only with a NASM package
  ([NASM blog](https://blog.nasm.org/nasm-edge/introducing-nasm-cpt-exam-prep-on-nasm-edge)). Best practice questions if bought.
- Book: _Flashcard Study System for the NASM Personal Trainer Exam_ (Mometrix, 2020) (in collection, grade A, id `f12a3e814e`)
  Use for: question styles only. The content contains errors (see NOTES.md). Never treat as an authority.

### Wider evidence (for "is NASM right about this?" and real client work)
- Book: _Science and Development of Muscle Hypertrophy_ (2021), Brad Schoenfeld (in collection, grade A, id `3cca0177ae`)
  Use for: checking the Phase 3 claims against the research literature.
- Book: _Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning_, 4th ed., NSCA (in collection, grade B, id `e960000885`)
  The NSCA's textbook; an independent second view on program design and assessment. Prose only.
- Book: _Science and Practice of Strength Training_ (2020), Zatsiorsky, Kraemer & Fry (in collection, grade A, id `a532717129`)
  Use for: periodization and maximal-strength theory.

## Wisdom (Communities)
- [r/personaltraining](https://www.reddit.com/r/personaltraining/)
  Working trainers discussing certs, clients and the business side. Use for: "how does this play out with real clients?", side-gig questions.
- Real-world: practice the overhead squat assessment and a first OPT Phase 1 workout on a willing friend before the exam.

## Gaps
- No 7th-edition text in the collection. The 4th ed. is close but out of date on naming and some content (e.g. 7th-ed. chapter
  structure, behavioral coaching). Mitigation: ground every lesson in nasm.org pages and the blueprint.
- No trustworthy free practice-question bank found yet. Lessons supply their own retrieval quizzes.
