What each domain covers (condensed)

1 · Basic & Applied Sciences and Nutrition (15%)

Nervous, muscular, skeletal, cardiorespiratory, endocrine and digestive systems; bioenergetics; levers, force, torque; planes of motion; muscle actions (isometric, concentric, eccentric); force couples (agonist, antagonist, synergist, stabilizer); length–tension; stretch-shortening cycle; reciprocal and autogenic inhibition; motor learning; macro/micronutrients, hydration, energy systems (ATP-PC, glycolytic, oxidative), EPOC, label reading, supplements, fad diets, weight-management physiology. (K1–K19)

2 · Client Relations & Behavioral Coaching (15%)

Rapport and communication, SMART and other goal types, expectation management, the Transtheoretical Model (Stages of Change), coaching methods, habit strategies, barriers to change, psychological responses to exercise. (K20–K27)

3 · Assessment (16%)

PAR-Q, medical and lifestyle history, risk factors; resting HR, blood pressure, waist-to-hip; body composition; static posture; movement assessments (overhead squat, single-leg squat, push, pull); kinetic chain checkpoints (ankles, knees, LPHC, shoulders, head); performance and cardio tests; special populations; when to refer out; when to reassess. (K28–K44)

4 · Program Design (20%)

Periodization (macro/meso/microcycles; OPT levels and phases; linear vs. undulating); specificity, overload, variation; General Adaptation Syndrome; flexibility, resistance, cardio, core, balance, reactive (plyometric) and SAQ training, each with stabilization/strength/power exercise examples; acute variables; progressions/regressions; overtraining; fitness tech; special populations. (K45–K64)

5 · Exercise Technique & Training Instruction (24%)

Set-up and technique for every training type, warm-up and cool-down; kinesthetic, auditory and visual cueing; safety and intensity monitoring; signs to modify or stop; progressions/regressions; spotting; breathing; kinetic chain checkpoints. (K65–K72)

6 · Professional Development & Responsibility (10%)

Ethics and codes of conduct; scope of practice (no diagnosing, no psychological counseling, no meal plans); keeping the credential; marketing, client acquisition and retention, sales; equipment safety; emergency protocols (activate EMS); credible sources. (K73–K83)
