# Mission: Indian History

## Why
Three reasons, from the mission interview (2026-10-05): **heritage and family** (know where my family and culture come from),
**understanding India today** (its politics and religion keep arguing about the past), and **plain curiosity** (a "good for the
soul" subject, the opposite of my technical ones).

## Success looks like (a year from now)
- **Tell the whole arc:** explain Indus to the Republic to a friend, with the *causes* of the big turns, not just names and dates.
- **Judge history debates:** read a claim about the past in a newspaper or a political speech (Aryan migration, Mughal rule,
  Partition, ...) and say what the evidence supports, what historians disagree about, and what's simply wrong.

## Constraints
- Half subject: about 2 blocks a week out of the shared 1-hour-a-day floor (`notes/study-plan.md`).
- Reads the spine book (Kulke & Rothermund) as an EPUB with no page numbers: readings are assigned by section heading.
- Interested in every era (ancient, medieval/Mughal, colonial/freedom) and in cross-era themes (war and the state, economy, art,
  literature), so no era is cut; depth comes later through electives.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Reading sources in the original languages (Sanskrit, Persian): translations only.
- One-era specialism: the survey (IH 100) and the spine courses come first; a deep dive is a later elective, not a goal yet.
