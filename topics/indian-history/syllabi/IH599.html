Course description

An optional Level II capstone: a thesis of about 40 pages built on original research from primary sources, oral history, or both. I advise it the way a thesis advisor would, from proposal to final draft.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Carry out an extended research project on Indian history.
  2. Engage the historiography directly and take a position in it.
  3. Write a thesis to the standard of a master's degree.
