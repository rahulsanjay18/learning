## Course description
An optional elective in reading a source language at a basic level: either Sanskrit or Persian, your choice. The aim is modest
and practical. You'll learn the script, the core grammar, and enough vocabulary to follow a short, well-known passage with a
dictionary and a translation beside it, so you can check what translators do with a text.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Read and write the script (Devanagari or Perso-Arabic).
2. Explain the core grammar: Sanskrit noun and verb inflection and sandhi, or Persian verb forms and the ezafe.
3. Parse a short passage with a dictionary and compare two published translations of it.

## Texts
- **Sanskrit track (to buy):** Antonia Ruppel, *The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit* (2017), the first ten chapters.
- **Persian track (to buy):** Wheeler Thackston, *An Introduction to Persian*, 4th ed. (2009), the first ten lessons.
