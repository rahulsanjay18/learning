## Course description
An optional conversation at the end of Level I. You put together a list of 20 to 25 works you've read: the textbooks, primary
sources, and literature such as Manto. You write a short rationale for how they fit together, around 1,500 to 2,500 words. Then
we talk for an hour about how they connect and about a big question of your choice.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Explain how the works on your list relate across eras and kinds of evidence.
2. Defend a position on a big question in Indian history and answer objections.
3. Describe how your understanding changed during Level I.

## Texts
- **Required:** your own list of works.
