## Course description
An optional conversation at the end of Level II, like the Level I conversation but over graduate reading: historiography,
primary sources and regional histories. You write a list of works with a rationale, and we talk for an hour.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Explain how the graduate works you read connect.
2. Defend a historiographical position and answer objections.
3. Describe how your view of Indian history changed from Level I to Level II.

## Texts
- **Required:** your own list of works.
