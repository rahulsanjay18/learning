## Course description
An optional Level I capstone: a research essay of about 20 pages on a question you choose, using several secondary sources and
at least a few primary ones. I advise it the way a thesis advisor would: we agree on the question, then go through a
bibliography, an outline, a draft and a final version.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Frame a historical question that the sources can answer.
2. Build a bibliography and evaluate its sources.
3. Write a long argued essay that handles counter-evidence fairly.

## Texts
- **Required:** chosen with the question.
