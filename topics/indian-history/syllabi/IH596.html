Course description

An optional research practicum. You pick an original question that can be answered from primary sources in translation, or from your own oral-history material, and carry it through to a research paper of about 15 pages. It's the step from reading history to writing it.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Find and evaluate primary sources on a narrow question.
  2. Place your question in the existing historiography.
  3. Make an original argument from primary evidence.
