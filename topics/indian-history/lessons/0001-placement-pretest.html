Ancient

Which two cities are the best-known sites of the Indus (Harappan) civilization?

Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, excavated in the 1920s, revealed a Bronze Age urban civilization in the Indus valley.

In what language were the Vedas composed?

Vedic Sanskrit, the oldest form of Sanskrit; the texts were transmitted orally for centuries.

Who founded the Maurya Empire, by overthrowing the Nanda dynasty?

Chandragupta Maurya (late 4th century BCE). Chandragupta II is a later Gupta king with the same name.

Ashoka's remorse after which war led him to promote dhamma and Buddhism?

The Kalinga war. Ashoka's own inscriptions describe the deaths and deportations it caused.

Medieval

In which century was the Delhi Sultanate founded (by Qutb-ud-din Aibak and his successors)?

The early 13th century: Aibak declared independence from the Ghurids in 1206.

In what year did Babur defeat Ibrahim Lodi at Panipat, beginning Mughal rule? (Within two years counts.)

1526, the First Battle of Panipat. Babur's field artillery and muskets were decisive.

Which Mughal emperor is known for religious tolerance and an attempt to found a new syncretic faith?

Akbar. His "bold attempt at creating a new, tolerant religion died with him" (Kulke & Rothermund).

Colonial

In what year did Robert Clive win the Battle of Plassey in Bengal? (Within three years counts.)

1757. Clive defeated the nawab of Bengal and installed Mir Jafar; it began the East India Company's territorial power.

What was the main political result of the uprising of 1857?

In 1858 the East India Company was dissolved and India came under the British Crown.

Gandhi's 1930 march to the sea protested a British tax on what?

Salt: the 1930 salt march launched a civil disobedience campaign.

Independence

In 1947 British India was partitioned into which two states?

India and Pakistan (Pakistan on 14 August, India on 15 August). Bangladesh was East Pakistan until 1971.

In what year did the Constitution of India come into force, making India a republic?

1950 (26 January, now Republic Day). Rajendra Prasad became the first president.

Put these in chronological order, earliest first.

Indus cities (3rd–2nd millennium BCE) → Maurya (4th–2nd century BCE) → Delhi Sultanate (1206) → Mughals (1526) → Plassey (1757) → Independence (1947).
