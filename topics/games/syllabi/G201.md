## Course description
Xiangqi, Chinese chess. It shares an ancestor with chess, so you learn it mostly by transfer, and the interesting part is what
doesn't transfer. The cannon captures by jumping. The palace confines the general. Elephants can't cross the river. The two
generals may not face each other on an open file. Each of these changes the strategy, and each is a design choice worth
understanding.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Play a full game of xiangqi with all the rules, including the flying-general rule and perpetual-check rules.
2. Use the cannon in attack and defence, including the cannon screen.
3. Give the basic mating patterns.
4. Explain three ways xiangqi differs strategically from chess, and why the rules produce those differences.
5. Write a design notebook entry on the palace or the river.

## Texts
