## Course description
An optional conversation at the end of Level II, after the capstone. We talk through your game: the decisions behind it, the
evidence for each, and what you'd do differently.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Defend the major design decisions in your game with evidence.
2. Connect them to the theory and games you studied.
3. Say what you'd change in a second version.

## Texts
- **Required:** your design report.
