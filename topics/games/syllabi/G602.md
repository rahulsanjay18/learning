## Course description
A Level II course on balance: making a game fair, with no dominant strategy. It covers symmetric and asymmetric balance,
first-move advantage and how games fix it (komi, the swap rule, handicaps), finding dominant strategies, and measuring balance
with data. The most important source is a 2020 DeepMind study that used AlphaZero to test rule changes to chess. You'll do the
same for your variant, using the agents from G504.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Tell symmetric from asymmetric balance and give examples of each.
2. Compare the main fixes for first-move advantage and choose one for a given game.
3. Find a dominant strategy in a game and redesign the rule that creates it.
4. Measure balance from self-play data with confidence intervals.
5. Run a rule-variant experiment on your game and recommend a change.

## Texts
- **Required (in your library):** Sellers, *Advanced Game Design*, Chapters 9 "Game Balance Methods" and 10 "Game Balance Practice".
- **Required (to buy):** Ian Schreiber and Brenda Romero, *Game Balance* (CRC, 2021), selected chapters.
- **Paper:** Tomašev, Paquet, Hassabis and Kramnik, "Assessing Game Balance with AlphaZero: Exploring Alternative Rule Sets in Chess" (arXiv, 2020).
