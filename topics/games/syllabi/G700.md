## Course description
The Level II capstone: design, prototype, playtest and balance your own 3D strategy game. You play it with friends, balance it
from data using your agents, revise, and repeat. The final result is a rulebook, a playable version, and a design report on what
you changed and why.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Run playtests that produce useful data, not just impressions.
2. Balance a game from human playtests and from self-play.
3. Write a clear rulebook that a new player can learn from.
4. Write a design report that explains each major decision and its evidence.

## Texts
- **Required:** your design notebook and the outputs of G601–G603.
- **Recommended (in your library):** Sellers, *Advanced Game Design*, Chapters 11 "Working as a Team" and 12 "Making Your Game Real".
