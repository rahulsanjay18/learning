## Course description
An optional conversation at the end of Level I. You list the works you read and the games you learned, write a short rationale for
how they fit together, and we talk for an hour about what you now think strategy is.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Explain how the games, theories and strategic writings connect.
2. Defend a view of what makes strategy general across games.
3. Describe how your plan for the 3D game changed during Level I.

## Texts
- **Required:** your own list of works and your design notebook.
