## Course description
An optional practice log that runs alongside every game course. For each course you play a few real games, against bots or
people, and review at least one in writing. It's the best evidence that a lesson worked, since puzzles test recognition and games
test whether you use the idea when nobody tells you to.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Keep a log of games played in each family.
2. Review a game in writing: the turning point, the missed ideas, and the lesson it connects to.
