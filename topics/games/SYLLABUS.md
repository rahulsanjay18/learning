# Games syllabus

Planned ahead (2026-10-06). Course-level map: `PROGRAM.md`. This file is the **lesson-level** plan for the two active classes.
Objectives are graded **Got it / Not yet** (mastery rule: `TEACHING-LOG.md` rule 20). In chess, "got it" means you do it **in your own
games** (game reviews are the test), not only in puzzles. Games is a half major: about 2 blocks a week, shared between G101 and G150.

## G101 Chess, the ongoing lab (active): 12 lessons, in `topics/chess/lessons/`

Books: Seirawan & Silman, *Winning Chess Tactics* (id 50634c974d) · Seirawan, *Winning Chess Endings* (id b7c9d62e2b).
Game reviews (/game-review) slot in whenever you play a game; they don't use up a syllabus slot.

| # | Lesson | Reading | Objectives: you can… |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0001 | Is it safe? | — | done |
| 0002 | Checks first | — | done |
| 0003 | What did their move do? | — | before moving, list what the opponent's last move attacks (41. Rd2 from the 6 Oct game) |
| 0004 | What did my move stop defending? | — | before moving, list what your moving piece was guarding (13. e4 from the 2 Oct game) |
| 0005 | The double attack (fork) | *Tactics* Ch. 2 "The Double Attack" | find knight, queen and pawn forks in 3 moves or fewer |
| 0006 | Pins and skewers | *Tactics* Ch. 3 "The Pin", Ch. 4 "The Skewer" | tell an absolute from a relative pin · exploit a pin (pile up on it) |
| 0007 | King tactics and the back rank | *Tactics* Ch. 5 "King Tactics and Combinations" | spot back-rank weakness on both sides · give your king "luft" in time |
| 0008 | Deflection and decoy | *Tactics* Ch. 6 "Deflection", Ch. 9 "The Decoy" | find a deflection of an overloaded defender |
| 0009 | King and pawn endings | *Endings*, king-and-pawn chapter (opposition, distant opposition) | win K+P vs. K with the opposition · know when it's a draw |
| 0010 | Batteries and open files | *Tactics* Ch. 7 "Battery on an Open File or Diagonal" | double rooks / queen+bishop on a line and use it |
| 0011 | Zwischenzug and review mix | *Tactics* Ch. 12 "Zwischenzug" | check for an in-between move before recapturing |
| 0012 | G101 check | — | a mixed tactics set (timed, recall not recognition) + review of one recent game of yours |

Then G102 Tiny games (4 lessons: game trees, minimax by hand, Nim, Hex), with chess continuing as the lab.

## G150 Strategy foundations (active): 8 lessons, in `topics/military-strategy/lessons/`

Framed as decision systems under uncertainty (your preference, NOTES.md). Spine: Echevarria, *Military Strategy: A Very Short
Introduction* (id 940de01b70).

| # | Lesson | Reading | Objectives: you can… |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0001 | Capacity or will | Echevarria Ch. 1 "What is military strategy?" | done |
| 0002 | Annihilation vs. exhaustion | Echevarria Ch. 2 "Annihilation and dislocation", Ch. 3 "Attrition and exhaustion" | classify a campaign on capacity/will × fast/slow · fix: offense ≠ annihilation (First Punic War) |
| 0003 | Deterrence and coercion | Echevarria Ch. 4 "Deterrence and coercion" | tell deterrence from compellence · explain why a threat must be credible (a game-theory link) |
| 0004 | Clausewitz | *On War* Bk 1 Ch. 1 (free Graham translation, clausewitzstudies.org) | explain "war is a continuation of policy" · explain friction and the trinity |
| 0005 | Sun Tzu and the indirect approach | *The Art of War* (Giles) Ch. I "Laying Plans", II "Waging War", III "Attack by Stratagem" | compare Sun Tzu with Clausewitz on one point · say what "winning without fighting" means as a strategy |
| 0006 | Decapitation and terror as strategies | Echevarria Ch. 5 "Terror and terrorism", Ch. 6 "Decapitation and targeted killing" | explain why these strategies usually fail to reach their political aims (analysed, not glorified) |
| 0007 | Why strategies succeed or fail | Echevarria Ch. 8 "What causes military strategies to succeed or fail?" | apply the book's criteria to one campaign you know |
| 0008 | G150 check: a campaign analysis | Polybius Book III (Hannibal), slices given then | a ~500-word analysis: ends–ways–means, strategy type, why it worked or failed |

Optional extra: Echevarria Ch. 7 "Cyber power and military strategy".
