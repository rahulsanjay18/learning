Course description

What a staff engineer is and how people become one. We read Part I of Tanya Reilly's The Staff Engineer's Path alongside Will Larson's Staff Engineer. The topics are the shape of the role (scope, primary focus, the four archetypes), how to see your organization clearly (Reilly's three maps), and how to choose work that matters. Your placement put you at the tech-lead level aiming at the Solver archetype, so the exercises lean that way.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Describe your role's scope, shape and primary focus in Reilly's terms, and get your manager to agree with the description.
  2. Draw Reilly's three maps (locator, topographical, treasure) for your organization.
  3. Use Larson's "work on what matters" test to choose between two projects.
  4. Start and keep a brag document with evidence of staff-level impact.

Texts

  • Required (in your library): Reilly, The Staff Engineer's Path (O'Reilly, 2022), Part I: Chapter 1 "What Would You Say You Do Here?", Chapter 2 "Three Maps", Chapter 3 "Creating the Big Picture".
  • Required (in your library): Larson, Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track (2021): "Does the title even matter?", "Operating at Staff", "Work on what matters".
  • Optional: Julia Evans, "Get your work recognized: write a brag document" (jvns.ca, free).
