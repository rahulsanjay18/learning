Course description

An optional track: embedded and robotics software. It uses your computer engineering degree, and edge AI (running models on devices) connects it to your current work. The path is CP101 and CP120, then embedded operating systems and design, and a microcontroller project.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Explain how embedded operating systems handle scheduling, interrupts and memory.
  2. Design embedded software with real-time constraints.
  3. Run a small model on a microcontroller and measure it.

Texts

  • Required (in your library): Holt, Embedded Operating Systems. Ganssle, The Art of Designing Embedded Systems.
  • Required (free): TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers documentation.
