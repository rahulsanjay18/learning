## Course description
A baseline for the interview track at the big-tech staff level. In one long meeting you solve a timed coding problem in Vim, tell
one leadership story at staff scope, and outline a deep dive on a past project. Then you rate yourself against a staff rubric. The
results decide which round types IV100 practices most.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. List the round types in a big-tech staff loop and what each one grades.
2. Solve one medium coding problem in 35 minutes in Vim, talking through trade-offs.
3. Tell a staff-scope leadership story in about three minutes.

## Texts
- **Required:** a current guide to the Google L6 and Meta E6 interview loops (Hello Interview's guides are free).
- **Recommended (in your library):** Skiena, *The Algorithm Design Manual*, the problem catalog.
