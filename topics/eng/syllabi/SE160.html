Course description

Turning evidence into a title, by either of the two routes: a promotion packet where you work, or an offer at the staff level elsewhere. We use Larson's chapters on promotion packets and sponsors and Reilly's Part III. The course also covers leveling conversations and negotiation. Interview practice itself happens in the interview track.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Write a promotion packet that maps your evidence to a staff rubric.
  2. Find a sponsor and work with them.
  3. Build a story bank of staff-scope projects for interviews.
  4. Discuss leveling with a manager or recruiter, and negotiate an offer.

Texts

  • Required (in your library): Larson, Staff Engineer: "Getting the title where you are", "Promotion packets", "Find your sponsor", "Staff projects", "Present to executives".
  • Required (in your library): Reilly, The Staff Engineer's Path, Part III: Chapter 7 "You're a Role Model Now (Sorry)", Chapter 8 "Good Influence at Scale", Chapter 9 "What's Next?".
  • Required (in your library): Voss, Never Split the Difference, the chapters on calibrated questions and bargaining.
