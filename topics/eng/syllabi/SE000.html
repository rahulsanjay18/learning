Course description

A placement for the staff track, completed October 8, 2026. You rated yourself against a public staff rubric with evidence, named your closest staff archetype, and drafted a design for the WebSocket move relay (3dChessServer #13) in 25 minutes. Result: tech lead at team scope, aiming at the Solver archetype. There's no written strategy or cross-team conflict work yet. No course was shortened, and system design depth (SE120) gets extra weight.

Learning objectives

  1. Name Larson's four staff archetypes and say which one your current work most resembles, with evidence.
  2. Rate yourself on six staff dimensions with evidence, and name your two biggest gaps.
  3. Draft a staff-depth design for a real system in 25 minutes.

Texts

  • Larson's staff archetypes (staffeng.com) and a public engineering career framework.
