Course description

An optional course on mentoring, sponsoring and raising the engineering bar: being a role model, teaching through code and design review, and growing other engineers into leads. You already mentor, so the course is about doing it more deliberately.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Explain the difference between mentoring and sponsoring, and do both on purpose.
  2. Use code and design review to teach, not only to catch defects.
  3. Create space for others to lead.

Texts

  • Required (in your library): Reilly, The Staff Engineer's Path, Chapter 7 "You're a Role Model Now (Sorry)" and Chapter 8 "Good Influence at Scale". Larson, Staff Engineer, "Create space for others" and "Build a network of peers".
