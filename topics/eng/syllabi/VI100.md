## Course description
Vim, learned one tip at a time. Every engineering lesson opens with a one-minute tip from Drew Neil's *Practical Vim*, in order,
showing the exact keys and what each does. Trying it is optional. If you send me your keystrokes, I replay them and tell you
where they went wrong. The course has no meetings of its own. Setup is the first step: Vim as your git editor, and Vim keybindings
in any IDE you use.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Edit with Vim's grammar of operators and motions instead of the arrow keys.
2. Use the dot command, text objects, registers and macros on real edits.
3. Do all the coding reps in the engineering courses in Vim.

## Texts
- **Required (in your library):** Drew Neil, *Practical Vim*, 2nd ed. (Pragmatic Bookshelf, 2015), one tip per lesson.
- **Required:** `vimtutor`, once, at the start.
