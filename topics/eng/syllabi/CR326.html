Course description

An optional math toolkit course: convex optimization and linear and integer programming. It strengthens every math-based fallback (quant, operations research, ML) and is useful in ML work directly. Operations research analyst as a job title pays less than your current path, so treat this as a skill, not a destination.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Recognize convex problems and formulate them in standard form.
  2. Explain duality and the KKT conditions.
  3. Formulate and solve linear and integer programs with a solver (OR-Tools).

Texts

  • Required (in your library): Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization (Cambridge, 2004): Chapters 1 "Introduction", 2 "Convex sets", 3 "Convex functions", 4 "Convex optimization problems", 5 "Duality".
  • Required (free): Google OR-Tools documentation, linear and integer programming guides.
