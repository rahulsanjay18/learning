Course description

An optional fallback path: cloud or AI solutions architect, a customer-facing role that pays well and values certifications. The path is the AWS Solutions Architect – Associate (you already have a 2024 course for it), then the Professional level, or Google's Professional Cloud Architect.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Design well-architected solutions on AWS for given requirements.
  2. Explain trade-offs to a non-specialist customer.
  3. Pass the Solutions Architect – Associate exam.

Texts

  • Required: your 2024 AWS Solutions Architect – Associate course (in your library); the AWS Well-Architected Framework (free); the current SAA exam guide.
