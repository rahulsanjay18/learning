## Course description
An optional safety net outside tech: becoming an actuary through the Society of Actuaries exams. Getting in means passing a series of exams over several years. The work is remote-friendly and outside the software job market, and the sign-off work is the part least exposed to automation. The first step, Exam P (probability), overlaps heavily with the Statistics major. This course is a probe: prepare for and take Exam P, then decide.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Solve Exam P problems in the time the exam allows.
2. Decide, with evidence, whether to continue to Exam FM.

## Texts
- **Required:** the SOA's current Exam P syllabus and sample questions (free at soa.org). A Exam P study manual of your choice (to buy).
- **Recommended:** your S201–S205 notes from the Statistics major.
