## Course description
Machine learning systems in production and the operations work that keeps them running (MLOps), from all of Chip Huyen's
*Designing Machine Learning Systems*: data engineering, training data, feature engineering, evaluation, deployment, distribution
shift and monitoring, continual learning, infrastructure, and the human side. It's the first step toward ML infrastructure and
platform roles. The deliverable is a public design document plus a small pipeline repository (MLflow, Docker, CI) built on your RL agent.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Frame a business problem as an ML problem, with objectives and constraints.
2. Design data pipelines and training data, including labeling, sampling and class imbalance.
3. Choose offline evaluation methods that predict online performance.
4. Choose between online and batch prediction, and between model compression options.
5. Detect and respond to distribution shift with monitoring.
6. Design continual learning and testing in production (shadow, A/B, canary, bandits).
7. Lay out the infrastructure an ML team needs, from storage to orchestration.

## Texts
- **Required (in your library):** Chip Huyen, *Designing Machine Learning Systems* (O'Reilly, 2022): Chapters 1 "Overview of Machine Learning Systems", 2 "Introduction to Machine Learning Systems Design", 3 "Data Engineering Fundamentals", 4 "Training Data", 5 "Feature Engineering", 6 "Model Development and Offline Evaluation", 7 "Model Deployment and Prediction Service", 8 "Data Distribution Shifts and Monitoring", 9 "Continual Learning and Test in Production", 10 "Infrastructure and Tooling for MLOps", 11 "The Human Side of Machine Learning".
- **Recommended (in your library):** Kleppmann, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, the data-system chapters.
