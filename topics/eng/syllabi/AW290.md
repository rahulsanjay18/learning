## Course description
Final preparation for the MLA-C02 exam and the exam itself: timed practice exams, review of every missed question by domain, and
the real exam. The exam is 170 minutes long.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Score consistently above the passing mark on full-length timed practice exams.
2. Explain the right answer to every question you missed.
3. Pass the MLA-C02 exam.

## Texts
- **Required:** AWS, *MLA-C02 Exam Guide*; AWS Skill Builder's official practice question set; any practice exams you supply.
