## Course description
Writing is most of a staff engineer's leverage. This course covers design documents, RFCs and architecture decision records: how
to structure them, how to get them reviewed, and how to make a decision stick. Each lesson ends with a real document for one of
your projects. The course deliverable is one design document taken through review, plus one decision record.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Write a design document with clear goals, non-goals, alternatives considered and a recommendation.
2. Write an architecture decision record that someone can understand a year later.
3. Run a document review: choose reviewers, collect comments, resolve disagreements, and record the decision.
4. Write prose that busy readers finish: the main point first, short paragraphs, concrete examples.
5. Recognize when a decision needs a document and when it doesn't.

## Texts
- **Required (free at abseil.io):** Winters, Manshreck and Wright, *Software Engineering at Google* (O'Reilly, 2020): Chapter 3 "Knowledge Sharing", Chapter 10 "Documentation", Chapter 9 "Code Review".

- **Optional (in your library):** Stephen King, *On Writing*, the "Toolbox" section.
