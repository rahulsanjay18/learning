## Course description
A Level II course on C++ templates and metaprogramming, from Vandevoorde, Josuttis and Gregor's *C++ Templates: The Complete
Guide* (2nd ed.): the full rules of templates, variadic templates, compile-time programming, type traits, SFINAE and enable_if,
the polymorphic power of templates, typelists, tuples and expression templates.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Explain template argument deduction, name lookup and instantiation precisely.
2. Write variadic templates and fold expressions.
3. Do compile-time computation with constexpr and templates.
4. Implement type traits, and use SFINAE, enable_if and concepts to control overloads.
5. Choose between static and dynamic polymorphism and bridge them.
6. Implement a typelist, a tuple and an expression template.

## Texts
- **Required (to buy):** Vandevoorde, Josuttis and Gregor, *C++ Templates: The Complete Guide*, 2nd ed. (2017): Part I "The Basics" (Chapters 1–11) and Part III "Templates and Design" (Chapters 18–28, selected).
- **Not used:** the 1st edition (2002) in your library, which predates C++11.
