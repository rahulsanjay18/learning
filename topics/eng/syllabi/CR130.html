Course description

Fine-tuning and post-training large language models at scale: supervised fine-tuning, parameter-efficient methods (LoRA), preference tuning (RLHF and DPO), distributed training (DDP and FSDP), and evaluation. These are among the best-paid AI skills right now. We build up from Raschka's from-scratch implementation to multi-GPU training. The deliverable is a public fine-tuning repository with distributed training and evaluations, and the NVIDIA NCP-GENL certification if it's bookable by then.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Implement and explain a GPT-style model, its pretraining loop and its decoding strategies.
  2. Fine-tune a model for classification and for instruction following, and evaluate the result.
  3. Use LoRA and quantized fine-tuning and explain the memory savings.
  4. Explain RLHF and DPO, and run DPO on a preference dataset.
  5. Train across several GPUs with DDP and FSDP, and explain where memory goes.
  6. Reproduce your RL agent's training run with a documented, repeatable recipe.

Texts

  • Required (in your library): Sebastian Raschka, Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch) (Manning, 2024): Chapters 4 "Implementing a GPT model from scratch to generate text", 5 "Pretraining on unlabeled data", 6 "Fine-tuning for classification", 7 "Fine-tuning to follow instructions".
  • Required: NVIDIA, NCP-GENL exam guide and study path. PyTorch documentation on distributed training and FSDP.
  • Optional (in your library): Hwu, Kirk and El Hajj, Programming Massively Parallel Processors, if CUDA kernels come up.
