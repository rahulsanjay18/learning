Course description

An optional fallback path, on hold: quantitative research. It has the highest ceiling of the math-based fallbacks, but top firms treat a PhD as nearly a baseline, so the realistic targets are mid-tier funds and systematic trading teams. The path uses the Statistics major (probability, inference, time series) plus probability-puzzle interview preparation and convex optimization.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Solve quant-interview probability and brainteaser problems under time pressure.
  2. Explain the basics of portfolio optimization as a convex problem.
  3. Say which parts of the Statistics major a quant research role uses.

Texts

  • Required (to buy): Xinfeng Zhou, A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews (2008). Timothy Crack, Heard on the Street.
  • Recommended (in your library): Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Chapters 1–4.
