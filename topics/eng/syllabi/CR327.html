Course description

An optional safety net outside tech: patent agent. Your computer engineering and computer science degrees qualify you to sit the USPTO registration exam without a law degree. Patent agents prepare and prosecute patent applications, and filings must carry a registered practitioner's name. This course is a probe: read the exam's scope and take one practice exam, then decide.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Describe what the exam covers and how it's structured.
  2. Answer practice questions using the Manual of Patent Examining Procedure.
  3. Decide, with evidence, whether to pursue it.

Texts

  • Required (free): USPTO, General Requirements Bulletin for Admission to the Examination; the Manual of Patent Examining Procedure (MPEP), the chapters the exam emphasizes; USPTO's released past exams.
