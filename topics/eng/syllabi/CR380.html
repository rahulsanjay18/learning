Course description

Platform engineering and site reliability engineering, from Google's Site Reliability Engineering book, aimed at the Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) exam. It covers service level objectives and error budgets, monitoring and alerting, on-call and incident response, postmortems, handling overload, and running Kubernetes clusters rather than just deploying to them.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Define SLIs and SLOs for a service and use an error budget to make release decisions.
  2. Design monitoring and alerting that pages only for real problems.
  3. Run incident response and write a blameless postmortem.
  4. Design against overload and cascading failures.
  5. Install, upgrade, troubleshoot and secure a Kubernetes cluster at the level the CKA tests.

Texts

  • Required (free at sre.google): Beyer, Jones, Petoff and Murphy (eds.), Site Reliability Engineering (O'Reilly, 2016): Chapter 3 "Embracing Risk", 4 "Service Level Objectives", 5 "Eliminating Toil", 6 "Monitoring Distributed Systems", 10 "Practical Alerting", 14 "Managing Incidents", 15 "Postmortem Culture", 21 "Handling Overload", 22 "Addressing Cascading Failures".
  • Required (free): the CNCF CKA curriculum and the kubernetes.io documentation.
  • Recommended (in your library): Rosso et al., Production Kubernetes, Chapter 2 "Deployment Models" and Chapter 12 "Multitenancy".
