Course description

Leading large projects across teams when nobody reports to you. This is Part II of Reilly's book: choosing what to spend finite time on, starting and running big projects, and dealing with stuck projects, including deciding to end one. You already lead cross-team work, so the lessons sharpen habits you have rather than start from zero. The deliverable is a kickoff document and a retrospective for a real project.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Choose which projects to take on using an explicit budget of your time and energy.
  2. Kick off a cross-team project with clear goals, roles and a communication plan.
  3. Diagnose why a project has stalled and get it moving again.
  4. Stay aligned with the people who have authority over your work.
  5. End or hand off a project cleanly, with a retrospective.

Texts

  • Required (in your library): Reilly, The Staff Engineer's Path, Part II: Chapter 4 "Finite Time", Chapter 5 "Leading Big Projects", Chapter 6 "Why Have We Stopped?".
  • Required (in your library): Larson, Staff Engineer: "Stay aligned with authority", "To lead, you have to follow", "Managing technical quality".
  • Optional (in your library): Brooks, The Mythical Man-Month, the title essay.
