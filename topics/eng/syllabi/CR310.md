## Course description
An optional fallback path, on hold: quant developer. Top firms mostly hire on-site in a few cities, so this stays on watch rather than in rotation. If that changes, the path is the C/C++ sequence through CP204 and CP403 for low-latency performance, plus market microstructure and systematic trading.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this course you can:
1. Explain how exchanges and order books work and where latency matters.
2. Describe how a systematic trading system is built and tested.
3. Say what a quant developer interview tests and where your skills already fit.

## Texts
- **Required (in your library):** Larry Harris, *Trading and Exchanges: Market Microstructure for Practitioners* (Oxford, 2003), Part I.
- **Required (in your library):** Robert Carver, *Systematic Trading*.
