Course description

An optional certification course: AWS Certified Generative AI Developer – Professional (AIP-C01). It's the professional-level step after the MLA-C02 associate exam and focuses on building generative AI applications with Amazon Bedrock.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Design generative AI applications on AWS: Bedrock models, knowledge bases, agents and guardrails.
  2. Evaluate, secure and operate those applications at scale.
  3. Pass the exam.

Texts

  • Required (free): AWS, AIP-C01 exam guide; Amazon Bedrock documentation; AWS Skill Builder's exam preparation.
  • Recommended: your AW201 and CR120 notes.
