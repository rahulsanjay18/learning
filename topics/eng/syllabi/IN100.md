## Course description
The Friday track, for topics you find interesting whether or not they pay off. It holds one active topic and two queued. New ideas
go on the ideas list and only come in when something finishes. Reps are optional here. If a topic turns out to matter for your
career, it moves into the tech track.

## Learning objectives
For each topic, by the end you can:
1. Explain the topic's core idea to another engineer.
2. Connect it to one of your projects, usually 3D chess.

## Texts
- **Active, how chess engines search:** the Chess Programming Wiki (free): "Alpha-Beta", "Move Ordering", "Transposition Table", "NNUE".
- **Queued, AlphaZero and MuZero as papers:** Silver et al., *Science* 362 (2018); Schrittwieser et al., *Nature* 588 (2020).
- **Queued, measuring your variant:** your own engine.
