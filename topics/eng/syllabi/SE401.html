Course description

A Level II course for after the staff title: the principal engineer's job of setting direction across an organization. Readings will be chosen when you start, from Larson's later work on engineering strategy and from principal engineers' published writing.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you can:

  1. Write strategy that spans several organizations.
  2. Influence executives and the company's technical direction.
  3. Build the bench of staff engineers around you.

Texts

  • Required: to be chosen when the course starts. Candidates: Larson, Crafting Engineering Strategy (2025); Larson's staffeng.com interviews with principal engineers.
