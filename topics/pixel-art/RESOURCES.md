## Knowledge

### Courses and tutorials (free, high trust)
- [Course: Les Forges Pixel Art Course, by Fil Razorback (OpenGameArt translation)](https://opengameart.org/content/les-forges-pixel-art-course)
  8 short chapters: tools, lines & curves, perspective, light, palettes, anti-aliasing, dithering, tiles. Use for: the technical
  backbone of the course; each chapter maps to roughly one lesson.
- [Article: "Pixel Art Outlines", by Skeddles (Lospec)](https://lospec.com/articles/pixel-art-outlines/)
  Short, illustrated: diagonal-only joins, equal segments, graduated curves, deliberate corners. Use for: lines and outlines (lesson 1).
- [Blog: Pixelblog, by Raymond Schlitter / Slynyrd](https://www.slynyrd.com/pixelblog-catalogue)
  Long-running tutorial series by a professional pixel artist. Use for: #1 color palettes (HSB ramps), #5 basics (canvas size,
  scaling), #6 light & shadow, #8 intro to animation, #22 top-down character sprites, #25 motion cycles.
- [Tutorial: "Pixel Art Tutorial", by Derek Yu (creator of Spelunky)](https://derekyu.com/makegames/pixelart.html)
  Classic end-to-end sprite walkthrough (palette, jaggies, form, outlining, dithering). Use for: a first full sprite. (Couldn't fetch the
  page from the cloud container. The outline above comes from the [80.lv summary](https://80.lv/articles/pixel-art-basics-tutorial).)
- [Tutorials: Pedro Medeiros (saint11), pixel art tutorials](https://saint11.art/blog/pixel-art-tutorials/)
  Animated-GIF micro-tutorials by the Celeste artist. Use for: quick visual demos, especially animation and effects.
- [Index: Lospec pixel art tutorials](https://lospec.com/pixel-art-tutorials)
  Searchable by tag (lines, shading, animation…). Use for: finding a second explanation of any topic.
- [Article: "Pixel Art Practices Explained Simply", by FrostDrive (Clip Studio Tips)](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/5360)
  Clear definitions of doubles and jaggies. Use for: terminology.

### Books
- Book: _Pixel Logic: A Guide to Pixel Art_, by Michael Azzi (check: [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/l/pixel-logic)). Visual, beginner-friendly,
  widely recommended (listed by Lospec). Not in collection; on library/WANTED.md.

### Software (open source)
- [LibreSprite (GPL-2.0)](https://github.com/LibreSprite/LibreSprite): the learner's editor. Fork of the last GPL Aseprite (2016). Installs:
  [Flathub](https://flathub.org/apps/com.github.libresprite.LibreSprite) (Linux), [GitHub releases](https://github.com/LibreSprite/LibreSprite/releases)
  (Windows), [browser version](https://libresprite.github.io/online/).
- [LibreSprite wiki: "Know your workspace"](https://github.com/LibreSprite/libresprite.github.io/wiki/Know-your-workspace): the official
  tool and shortcut tour. Use for: any "where is X in LibreSprite" question. Most Aseprite tutorials also apply.
- [Lospec palette list](https://lospec.com/palette-list): curated limited palettes. Use for: palette lessons.

## Wisdom (Communities)
- [r/PixelArt](https://www.reddit.com/r/PixelArt/): large, active. Use the critique ("CC") flair to get feedback on finished pieces.
- [Lospec Discord and community list](https://lospec.com/pixel-art-communities): friendly, all skill levels, with feedback channels.
- [Pixel Joint forum](https://pixeljoint.com/): long-running pixel-art community with a strict-critique culture. Use for: serious feedback once the basics are in place.
