# Starting point: no drawing background, wants game sprites and illustrations in LibreSprite

The learner has no drawing experience ("can't draw") and wants to make game sprites and pixel-art illustrations with
open-source software. They chose LibreSprite on Windows/Linux, with ~15-minute sessions.

**Evidence**: self-reported in the opening interview (2026-10-02).

**Implications**: lean on pixel art's mechanical rules (lines, clusters, palettes) before anything that needs
observational drawing. Every lesson gets a real LibreSprite task and a request for the PNG, so feedback comes from
their actual work. The learner's first exported PNG decides whether lesson 2 is more line practice or moves on to
silhouettes and outlines.
