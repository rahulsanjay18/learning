# Mission: Pixel art

## Why
Make my own game sprites and pixel-art illustrations, using open-source tools, starting from zero drawing ability.

## Success looks like
- I can draw clean outlines (no doubles or jaggies) for a character, object or tile at 16–32 px.
- I can make a small animated game sprite (idle and walk cycle) in LibreSprite and export a sprite sheet.
- I can build a limited palette and shade a sprite so it reads at 1× size.
- I can finish a small pixel-art illustration (a scene at ~128×128) and share it for critique.

## Constraints
- About 15 minutes per session. Inattentive ADHD: one idea per lesson, instant feedback.
- No drawing background. Skills come from rules and deliberate practice, not talent.
- Open-source software only: LibreSprite (GPL-2.0) on Windows/Linux.

## Out of scope
- Paid tools (Aseprite builds, Photoshop), digital painting, 3D and voxel art.
- Game programming. Making the art usable in a game engine comes later, if at all.
